YellowMarkets – Daily Deals & Flash Sales Enhancement Plan & Affected Files
We have inspected the database schema, Admin and Merchant controllers and views, shared pricing presenters, and storefront product card rendering components. Below is the comprehensive identification of affected files, architectural analysis, and step-by-step implementation plan for your review and approval before making any code changes.

1. Summary of Architectural Findings
Current Decoupled Workflow:

In Admin and Merchant panels, special pricing is handled independently under WebshopController (webshop/discount/*) which writes to the products_special_prices table.
Products table contains promotion columns (daily_deals, daily_deal_ends_at, flash_sale, flash_sale_ends_at, special_price, special_price_from, special_price_to), but the Edit Product form (edit_product.php) and Add Product form (add_product.php) lack a unified promotional configuration interface.
products currently lacks daily_deal_starts_at and flash_sale_starts_at columns to support scheduled activation based on calendar start times.
Scheduling & Automatic Activation/Expiry:

Currently, promotions only check end times via cron or runtime filters, without validating calendar start times (starts_at).
By storing start and end timestamps (daily_deal_starts_at, daily_deal_ends_at, flash_sale_starts_at, flash_sale_ends_at) and centralizing eligibility checks, promotions will automatically transition through Scheduled $\rightarrow$ Active $\rightarrow$ Expired $\rightarrow$ Disabled without manual product updates.
Storefront Card Consistency (Grid & Normal Layouts):

Storefront product cards across Trending Products, New Arrivals, category pages, search results, and deal pages route through ProductList.php $\rightarrow$ components/product_listing_page_item.php.
The current card does not feature the exact badges, discount percentage overlays, or live JavaScript countdown timers depicted in your two reference screenshots.
